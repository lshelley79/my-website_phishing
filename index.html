You've Been Tricked, Not Treated!

That Halloween surprise was actually a phishing simulation! No haunted house here—just a reminder that even fun-looking messages can hide a trick.

🍬 The real treat? Knowing what to watch for.

Unexpected prizes, mysterious coworkers, and “click before it's too late” deadlines are all ways scammers spark curiosity. Before following a link, check the sender, inspect the destination, and verify the message through a trusted channel.
